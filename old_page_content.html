Our PCB Design & Development Process


Our seamless process is designed to ensure that each project is completed on time and meets the highest standards. Here’s a breakdown of our PCB design and manufacturing workflow:


1. Consultation & Requirement Gathering


Every successful PCB development project begins with a thorough consultation. Our team works closely with you to understand the product’s functionality, design requirements, and performance goals. This phase is crucial for ensuring that the final PCB design aligns with your vision and technical needs.


2. Schematic Capture & Circuit Design


Once we have all the necessary information, we create a schematic diagram that outlines the electrical circuit of the PCB. This design serves as a blueprint for the PCB, detailing component placements and their interconnections.


3. PCB Layout & Routing


In the PCB layout phase, we strategically place components and define their routing paths. We ensure that the layout supports optimal electrical performance, minimizes heat buildup, and complies with design rules. For multi-layer boards, we carefully route connections between layers to ensure integrity and reliability.


4. Design Rule Check (DRC) & Simulation


We conduct thorough Design Rule Checks (DRC) to verify that the design complies with industry standards and is free from errors like inadequate trace width, spacing issues, or other potential design flaws. Additionally, we simulate the electrical performance of the design to verify signal integrity and thermal management.


5. Prototyping & Testing


Before proceeding with mass production, we manufacture a prototype of the PCB. The prototype undergoes a series of tests to validate its electrical and mechanical performance. If any issues are found during testing, we make necessary revisions before moving to production.


6. Mass Production & Delivery


Once the prototype is validated, we proceed with mass production. We ensure that the production process adheres to strict quality control measures to guarantee consistency and high performance across all units. We also offer flexible manufacturing volumes, from small batches to large-scale production.
